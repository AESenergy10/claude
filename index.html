05Auction Buying — We Bid For You

No auction account or license? View listings as a guest, send us the lot number, and we bid and buy on our licensed account for you.

Flat $500 buyer fee, any car — same fee whether the car sells under or over $5,000. You pay the winning price + the $500 fee.
Customer responsibility: you cover trucking from the auction to our warehouse. Auction "door"/storage fees (~$50–$100 / day) and any lay or late-payment fees are yours, not ours. If fees go unpaid over 5 days the auction can cancel the sale; cancellation penalty is $1,000–$1,500 (cars under $5,000) or $1,500–$2,000 (over $5,000).
06Shipping Terms & Payment

Delivery is guaranteed as long as your item is in our hands — no matter how long transit takes, you will get your item.

Once the container is sealed and on the vessel, transit time is out of our hands — schedule changes, port congestion and customs delays are the carrier's and customs authority's responsibility. No cancellation, refund or penalty once the container has sailed. Changes requested after sealing are billed to you.
